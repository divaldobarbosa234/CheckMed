DESCRIPTION — ENGLISH
Conny Cooper helps you organise prescriptions, schedules and dose reminders. Cooper is a companion character, not a clinician.

Photograph a prescription, import a PDF, or enter medicines manually. Review extracted text on your device before you save. Conny Cooper does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace your doctor or pharmacist.

Free includes your account, today’s path, reminders, and Taken. Taken stays free. Conny Cooper Super is an optional Apple subscription for extra room and premium features shown in the app. Prices, billing period, and any trial are shown by Apple before you buy. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID subscriptions. Restore Purchases is in the app.

Age 16+. No guest accounts. Support: https://connycooper.com/support/ and support@connycooper.com
Privacy: https://connycooper.com/privacy/
Terms: https://connycooper.com/terms/
